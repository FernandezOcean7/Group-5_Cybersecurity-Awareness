01 — INTRODUCTION

Understanding the Digital World

Why cybersecurity awareness matters in our everyday lives.

What is this website about?

As technology becomes an important part of everyday life, people increasingly rely on the internet for communication, education, entertainment, shopping, and financial activities. While these technologies provide convenience and opportunities, they also create opportunities for cybercriminals to deceive and exploit unsuspecting users.

This website focuses on phishing, online scams, and social engineering — common forms of digital deception that manipulate people into revealing personal information, giving away money, or providing unauthorized access to their accounts.

Through explanations, examples, realistic scenarios, and practical safety guidelines, this website aims to help readers understand how these threats work and how to respond to them responsibly.

02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

What Do We Aim to Achieve?

By exploring this website, readers should be able to:

01

Understand Cyber Threats

Explain phishing, online scams, and social engineering, including how cybercriminals use deception to target internet users.

02

Recognize Warning Signs

Identify suspicious messages, misleading links, fraudulent websites, fake accounts, and other common indicators of online scams.

03

Understand the Consequences

Recognize the possible financial, personal, emotional, and privacy-related consequences of becoming a victim of online fraud.

04

Practice Online Safety

Apply responsible online practices, protect personal information, secure digital accounts, and respond appropriately to suspicious activities.

05

Develop Critical Thinking

Evaluate online information and requests carefully instead of immediately trusting unfamiliar messages, offers, or individuals.

06

Promote Cybersecurity Awareness

Encourage responsible digital behavior and help friends, classmates, families, and communities become more aware of online threats.
